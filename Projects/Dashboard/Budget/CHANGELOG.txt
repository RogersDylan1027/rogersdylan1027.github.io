Version 0.6.1: Receipt-Date Fuel Tracking

Description:
Refines Car fill-up tracking so receipt dates drive the fuel averages, while Budget automatically calculates the user's average time between gas stops once there are at least two dated fill-ups.

Changes:
- Fill-Up Date defaults to today but remains editable before saving.
- Existing fill-ups can be edited later and moved to the correct receipt date.
- Fuel averages always use the saved Fill-Up Date, not the day the record was entered.
- Adds hidden created/updated timestamps for sync bookkeeping without affecting averages.
- Removes the manual average-duration selector once 2+ dated fill-ups exist.
- Automatically calculates average days between dated fill-ups.
- With 0 or 1 dated fill-up, shows the temporary Weekly / Every 2 Weeks / Monthly / Yearly / Every 2 Years duration choice.
- Average fill-up cost is calculated on the native average fill-up duration first.
- Only after the native duration is known does Budget scale the cost to the current Budget display period.
- Preserves 0.6.0 Car history, 2-of-3 receipt entry, weighted gas price, and Supabase sync.
