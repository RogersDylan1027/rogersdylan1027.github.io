Version 0.4.1: Local Statement Purchase Reset

Description:
Keeps Budget data local for now and changes Monthly Expenses so purchases are
kept only for the currently open credit-card statement. When the closing date
passes and a new statement cycle begins, purchases from the prior cycle are
removed automatically.

Changes:
- Removes the Budget-specific Supabase persistence dependency.
- Keeps existing Dashboard login/project-access protection.
- Preserves Main Budget and permanent Monthly Expenses.
- Monthly purchases are local-browser data only.
- Previous statement purchases are automatically deleted after the card closes.
- Removes the All Purchases/history view.
- Preserves existing Budget, Paycheck, Gas, CSV, settings, and user-created
  budget functionality.
