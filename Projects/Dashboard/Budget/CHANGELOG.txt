Version 0.4.0: Permanent Monthly Expenses

Description:
Adds Monthly Expenses as a second permanent Budget sheet alongside Main Budget.
Users set a monthly spending allocation and their credit-card statement closing
day, then record purchases in one ledger. The Monthly Expenses allocation is
always counted against Main Budget, while individual purchases track how much of
that allocation has been spent without double-counting the purchases in Main.

Changes:
- Adds permanent Monthly Expenses system budget.
- Main Budget and Monthly Expenses cannot be deleted.
- Monthly Expenses is always included in Main Budget.
- User chooses a monthly budget amount.
- User chooses a credit-card statement closing day from 1 through 31.
- Statement cycles automatically span from the day after the previous close
  through the next closing day, clamped correctly for shorter months.
- Adds purchases with date, description, amount, category, and notes.
- Shows Budgeted, Spent This Cycle, and Remaining totals.
- Adds Current Statement and All Purchases views so old purchases remain
  available as history.
- Adds complete Supabase persistence and Row Level Security.
- Preserves existing Main, Individual, Shared, Paycheck, Gas, CSV, and settings
  features.
- New Budget accounts start empty; no Paycheck or ChatGPT Plus sample data.
