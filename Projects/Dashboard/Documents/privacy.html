This Privacy Policy explains how My Dashboard ("My Dashboard," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you create an account, connect third-party services, and use Dashboard features.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, My Dashboard may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: your email address, authentication identifiers, account role, approval status, and related account settings.
  • Connected-account information: identifiers and authorization information necessary to connect services such as Google and Microsoft.
  • Calendar information: calendar names, event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, attendees, and other event details that a connected provider makes available to the Dashboard.
  • File and Drive information: file or folder names, identifiers, metadata, and content that you explicitly choose to access through supported file integrations.
  • Dashboard content: information you create in Dashboard projects and features, such as settings, project data, budgets, food logs, reviews, whiteboards, watchlists, streaming preferences, or similar user-created content.
  • Support and account-request information: account access requests, approval or decline status, decline reasons, support messages, and timestamps.
  • Device and app information: information needed for features such as Progressive Web App installation, notification permissions, push-subscription endpoints, and basic technical diagnostics.

My Dashboard is designed so that you choose which optional third-party accounts and features to connect.

2. Google User Data

If you connect a Google account, My Dashboard may request access only to Google services needed for the features you choose to use, such as Google Calendar and Google Drive.

Google data may be used to display, organize, refresh, or otherwise provide the Dashboard feature you requested. For example, Calendar access may be used to show events inside the Dashboard, and Drive access may be used to browse or work with files you choose to access.

My Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

My Dashboard does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell Google user data, and does not use Google user data to build advertising profiles.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate and improve My Dashboard, including to:

  • authenticate users and protect access to Dashboard accounts and projects;
  • provide features you request and synchronize supported connected services;
  • remember your Dashboard settings and preferences across devices where supported;
  • process account-access requests and administrator decisions;
  • send transactional messages, account notices, support messages, and notifications;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and protect the reliability of the service; and
  • develop and improve Dashboard features.

4. How Information Is Shared

My Dashboard does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the Dashboard, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, server functions, and related backend services;
  • Google when you choose to connect Google services;
  • Microsoft when you choose to connect supported Microsoft services;
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the Dashboard website; and
  • other providers that you explicitly choose to connect through a Dashboard feature.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or secure the service.

5. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

Dashboard information may be stored in Supabase, in your browser or device storage, or with the third-party provider that originally holds the information. Some integrations may use authorization tokens so the Dashboard can perform actions you request without requiring you to reconnect every time.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, including authenticated access and database access controls. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the feature or purpose for which it was collected, subject to technical, security, backup, and legal requirements. Certain temporary access features may have their own expiration periods.

6. Your Choices and Controls

You can control much of the information My Dashboard accesses by changing Dashboard settings or disconnecting a connected account. You may also revoke third-party permissions directly through the applicable provider, such as your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Revoking a provider connection may prevent related Dashboard features from working until the account is connected again.

If you want to request correction or deletion of Dashboard-stored account information, contact us using the address below. Some information may need to be retained where necessary for security, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

My Dashboard is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe such information has been provided, contact us so it can be reviewed.
